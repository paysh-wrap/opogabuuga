# Session 17 - "Two books + Markov" study on MNQ (research only, nothing built in the app)

Date 2026-11-11. Scripts in this folder (prep, core, feats, baseline, study_c1, study_c2, study_d, robustness, finish, report); raw outputs in `out/*.json`, trade streams in `out/streams/`. All tables below are regenerated by `python3 report.py` from those JSON files.

## What was and was not available
- **Books:** only the covers were provided. The rules used are the PUBLISHED rules: Connors & Alvarez, *Short Term Trading Strategies That Work* (2008): 200-day trend filter, RSI(2) < 5/10 (> 95/90 short) exit on close above/below the 5-day average, cumulative RSI(2) over 2 days < 35 (> 165) exit RSI(2) > 65 (< 35), Double 7's (7-day low above the 200-day -> exit at a 7-day high). *The Candlestick Trading Bible* (Homma-style teaching): pin bar (tail >= 2/3 of the range), engulfing bar, inside bar (trade the mother-bar break), only with the trend (EMA 8 > EMA 21 and close beyond EMA 21), only at a level (EMA-21 pullback, or prior-day high/low/close, today's open, VWAP), stop beyond the pattern, fixed 1.5/2/3 R target.
- **Markov video: NOT seen.** Only the generic Markov-property method was tested: daily states = terciles (IS cut-offs) of the day's return; transition table P(next RTH day up | state) fitted on 2019-05..2023-12 only, 1st order, 2nd order, x volatility regime, x opening gap; plus a 30-minute intraday chain. Tables frozen and applied unchanged to held-out data.

## Method (same as sessions 9-15)
- Data: Databento GLBX.MDP3 MNQ.v.0 1-minute bars 2019-05-06..2026-11-06 (RTH 09:30-16:00 ET), NQ.v.0 daily only for the 200-day warm-up. Back-adjusted prices for daily/TF indicators; trades on raw prices.
- Costs: 1 MNQ micro, $1.00 fee per round trip + 1 tick ($0.50) adverse slippage on every market/stop fill; limit targets need a 1-tick trade-through; stop first when stop and target share a 1-minute bar. Flat every day (no overnight).
- NOISE = verified noise-area momentum (`tools/noise_area.py` rules). The reproduced NOISE 1% stream is **identical trade-for-trade** to the session-9 stream (1,167 trades, +$22,464), so the harness is the verified one. "NOISE baseline" below = NOISE_250 (app default $250 emergency stop); NOISE_250_lock = preset C profit lock (+100 pts -> stop to +10).
- Protocol: everything chosen on IS 2019-05..2023-12, judged ONLY on HO 2024-01..2026-11.
- Pro 25K eval sim (sim25k assumptions): $1,250 target, $1,000 EOD trailing loss limit locking at 25,100, rolling starts every 2nd business day over HO, 120-day horizon. "strict" adds intraday breach on open-trade loss (MAE), the $600 daily loss limit and the 40 % best-day consistency on the pass.
- Reviewed the stopped helper's code before trusting it: all features are causal (daily bias uses the close of day i-1, entries 09:31 or later; Markov tables fitted on IS only; TF-bar signals enter on the next bar). No look-ahead or cost bug found. Two caveats found and handled: (a) every IS Markov state has P(up) >= 50 % (2019-23 bull market), so the "Markov bias" is in effect a long-only drift bet -> an always-long benchmark was added; (b) the eval-pass metric is noisy -> a random-thinning placebo, a permutation test and a day bootstrap were added (`finish.py`).

## Results in plain words
1. **Connors day-direction bias (T1):** no rule predicts the RTH day direction on HO (hit rates 38-53 %, all within noise of 50 %). The IS pick (Double 7's long-only) LOST $3,128 on HO, eval bust 67 %. Fail.
2. **Connors as NOISE filter (T2):** most "not AGAINST" filters are harmless but do not help; the IS pick (momentum guard: no NOISE short after RSI(2) > 90, no NOISE long after RSI(2) < 10) cut HO net (+$9,661 vs +$10,771) but improved eval pass/bust (54.1 / 41.9 vs 50.7 / 46.5). Random thinning to the same trade count gives 51.3 % pass on average (5-95 %: 37-65 %) -> not distinguishable on its own.
3. **Connors intraday (T3):** fails. IS pick (5m Double 7's, $100 stop, long only) -$3,287 on HO; the IS-profitable variants all lose or break even on HO; high win rates (60-69 %) but PF < 1 in-sample.
4. **Candlestick Bible intraday (T4):** fails. 96 variants: only 5 positive in both eras, 0 with HO PF >= 1.15. IS pick (15m inside bar at EMA-21, 3R) -$3,592 on HO, 35 % win.
5. **Markov day bias (T5):** IS hit 56-59 %, HO 50-52 % = the up-drift only. Best HO rows (+$7,741) are no better than simply going long every day (+$7,078, bust 70 %). IS pick (M1 x gap, 6 pts) -$774 on HO. Fail.
6. **Markov as NOISE filter / sizing (T6, T7):** filters raise PF but remove 40-60 % of trades -> pass rates fall to 14-52 %. Sizing up (2 micros when Markov agrees) raises net but busts 58-71 % of evals. Fail for Pro 25K.
7. **Intraday Markov 30m (T8):** fails (all HO rows negative).
8. **Combinations (T9):** adding any second book/Markov stream to NOISE (K1-K4) lowers $/trade and raises max DD; K4 shows 56 % pass only because extra variance speeds up both pass and bust (its added stream loses on HO). The only row that beats both baselines on HO is **K6 = NOISE + profit lock + momentum guard (RSI(2) 90/10)**: HO 368 trades, 52.4 % win, +$32.96/trade, PF 1.51, net +$12,128, max DD $1,546, eval PASS 65.2 % / BUST 30.6 % (strict 41.6 / 35.7) vs NOISE_250_lock 60.3 / 39.4 (strict 41.9 / 48.2) and NOISE_250 50.7 / 46.5.
9. **Robustness of K6 (T10 + finish.py):** every neighbour threshold 80/85/90/95 beats NOISE+lock on HO (pass 63-78 %, bust 22-37 %), both halves of the guard help on their own, and the blocked trades lose money in BOTH eras (IS 94 trades -$8.53/trade, HO 59 trades -$8.79/trade vs +$16.8 / +$27.2 for all trades). BUT: permutation p = 0.04 (IS) / 0.11 (HO); random thinning matches K6's pass rate 28 % of the time; HO net gain is only +$518 (day bootstrap 5-95 %: -$1,598..+$2,567, 33 % <= 0); by HO year the eval gain is 2026 (100 vs 87 % pass), 2025 small (+4.5 pts), 2024 none. In-sample eval sim (NOISE+lock 41.7 % pass / 14.5 % bust): the 90/10 guard gives 47.2 / 5.0, 95/5 gives 42.0 / 13.5, but 80/20 and 85/15 cut the pass rate to 34-36 % (bust 0-3 %) - fewer busts everywhere, pass rate not consistently higher.

## Ranked "combinations that could work" (plain language)
1. **NOISE + profit lock + "momentum guard"** (skip a NOISE short the day after a very strong up close, RSI(2) > 90; skip a NOISE long after a very weak close, RSI(2) < 10). Best held-out row, stable over thresholds, fewer busts. Evidence is WEAK (p ~ 0.1 on held-out, +$518 net), so: shadow/paper candidate only, re-check on new data before any app change.
2. **NOISE + profit lock alone** (already preset C) - still the solid reference; everything else in this study is worse than it.
3. Nothing else. Connors day bias, Connors intraday, candlestick patterns, Markov bias/filter/sizing, intraday Markov and all mixed streams fail on held-out data or lose to NOISE.

## Verdict vs the NOISE baseline
No book or Markov signal works as a stand-alone strategy on MNQ intraday with flat-daily rules and realistic costs. The single possible improvement is a tiny filter on NOISE (the RSI(2) momentum guard), which beats NOISE and NOISE+lock on held-out data but is not statistically proven. Recommendation: do NOT change the app; if wanted, run it in SHADOW next to NOISE and re-judge after >= 60 new sessions.

## Doubts / limits
- Rules from published descriptions only (books not read, video not seen); author-specific tweaks may differ.
- Eval-pass % depends on start dates and is noisy (rolling starts are overlapping, not independent).
- HO is one market regime (2024-26 bull with 2025 drawdown); 2025 was bad for every NOISE variant.
- Only MNQ RTH, 1 micro; MGC/MES not tested here.
