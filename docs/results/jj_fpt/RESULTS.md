# JJ F.P.T STRAT backtest (MNQ RTH, NY 09:30 session only)

Engine `app.engine.jj_fpt.replay` with default `JjFptCfg()`, unchanged; sizing with `trade_risk_usd` + `contracts_for` (D143, real
`config/rules/lucid_pro_{25,50,100,150}k.yaml`). Script `backtest_jj.py`, per-trade CSV `trades_jj.csv` (1 micro, every engine signal,
flag `taken_sequential`). Tables below were generated by the script.

Choices and limits (read first):
- Data is RTH only: 09:30 session only, no 08:30 news session, `htf_bias=None`. The missing 09:29 bar is a SYNTHETIC flat bar at the previous
  day's last close (first day: its own open), so the opening continuation is kept and "breaks the previous candle" means beyond the prior close.
- One `replay` per day over the full session; each signal is followed by the engine (stop first if a bar touches both). Signals still OPEN at the
  end (rare, target/stop not reached by 16:00) are closed at the 16:00 bar close.
- Costs per micro per round trip = yaml MNQ fee_rt $1.00 + 1 tick slippage per side ($1.00) = $2.00 (not the $1.50 of the brief). $2/pt.
- The engine's signals overlap (each is followed independently), so "ALL signals" is 3.8 trades/day; "Sequential" skips a signal arriving while
  a taken trade is open (max 1 position) and is what one account could take.
- Win % counts pts > 0 (a MTM close counts by its sign). PF and $/trade are after costs.
- Eval sim: intra-trade excursions are not modelled (a stopped trade loses exactly its stop); bust = closed balance at/below the trailing MLL.
  Sizing recomputed before every trade from DLL left, MLL room less $50 kill distance, $500 plan cap, consistency day stop (D143 formula).
  A trade needing < 1 micro is skipped (JJ_SIZE_BELOW_1), so a losing account stops trading instead of busting: "running/stalled", not "bust".

## Result in one paragraph
Measured win rate 37.7% (full) / 38.1% (last 12 months) against a break-even of 39.7% before costs and 41.3% after the $2 round-trip cost.
Profit factor 0.86 / 0.88, about -$3.8 / -$3.4 per trade at 1 micro; negative in every calendar year and in every entry kind except
CONTINUATION and BIG_OPEN, which are about break-even (PF 0.98 and 1.00 full; 1.07 and 1.08 in the last 12 months, small samples).
SMALL_ROOM is the weakest tier (PF 0.79). On the Lucid plans the rolling-start evals pass 3-5% (full) and 0% (last 12 months), 0% bust only
because the D143 sizing freezes the account near the MLL; the median unfinished account is deep in loss.
No sensitivity variant turns the edge positive.

Data: 1266 RTH days 2021-10-01..2026-10-30; 0 NaN minutes filled with a flat bar at the previous close. Costs per micro round trip: fee $1.00 + slippage 1 tick/side = $2.00 (MNQ in config/rules/lucid_pro_25k.yaml). $2/pt.
