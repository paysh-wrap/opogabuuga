## Verdict (honest rule: highest median AND mean, bust not > +2 pts vs fixed10 in either half)
- 25K R1: fixed10 (every other policy raises H2 bust by 6-17 pts; medians all 4,500).
- 50K R2: fixed10 (only asap_fullcap passes bust; its mean is higher, 16,038 vs 14,664, but median drops 13,070 -> 9,000).
- 100K R2: asap_>=50%cap (mean 18,434, median 18,985, bust 25.8 vs 26.0). Best mean is fixed2 (18,589).
- 150K Regular 3: asap_>=50%cap (mean 26,090, median 23,879, bust 23.5 = same).
- 150K High 4: fixed5 (mean 27,662, median 25,688, bust 27.9); every other faster policy fails the bust test.
- gap2 / gap3 / asap / fixed1 are identical: the cycle-profit minimum already takes >= 3 sessions, so a 2-3 session gap never binds.
- Notes: "take-home incl. survival" = cash paid only; survival = 100 - bust%. Bust is dominated by the 2024+ half (H2 42-70%), so differences are small vs sampling noise; Lucid's request-fail reasons for fixed cadences are ~95%+ of attempts (consistency 37-51%, remainder cycle profit not reached).
